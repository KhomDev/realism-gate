## Agreement

A blinded second pass (one blinded second AI reader (a read-only subagent) with the same rubric and source, no first-pass labels; not independent human validation) on a seeded 30-flag sample across both splits: raw agreement 29/30 = 96.7% [83.3-99.4], Cohen's kappa 0.9268; after adjudication 30/30 = 100.0% [88.6-100.0], kappa 1.0. Disagreements and their resolution are in `agreement.json`.

## What this cannot claim

- Not recall: a defect the rules do not flag is not counted.
- Not a property of later versions; one tree at one commit (1.0.4).
- Not predictive of judging outcomes; RQ3 is exploratory.
- Not independent human validation: one AI-assisted reader and one AI second pass.
- Flags are clustered within units and sources; the Wilson intervals assume independence and are too narrow for clustered data.
